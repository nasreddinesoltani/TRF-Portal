## Safety answer (why open categories cannot be affected)
The open/lightweight distinction lives on the BOAT records (`boatclasses.weightClass`: "open" | "lightweight" | "para"; unique index on discipline+code+weightClass — the duplicate-looking "1X"/"2X" records are by design). The "L" is inserted into a generated label ONLY when the specific race/result's boat record has weightClass "lightweight". Races on the open 1X/2X records (your W1x, M2x, BM1x, BW2x, bM1x…) are never touched, and the plan performs zero writes to the categories collection. The relabel data-fix targets exactly 7 documents by _id, each re-verified as "lightweight" before writing.
